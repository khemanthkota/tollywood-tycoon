Privacy Policy

Cinema Tycoon Bid
Android package: com.cinematycoonbid.game

Effective date: October 16, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes the Android game Cinema Tycoon Bid and its companion web game. For questions about privacy or data handling, contact the Cinema Tycoon Bid developer at cinematycoonbid@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores the player names or nicknames you enter, game settings, bids, purchases, movie selections, results, and photo preferences locally on your device using browser or WebView storage. This lets you resume games. You may use nicknames. The game does not send these entries to a developer-operated game server or synchronize games between players' devices. Anyone with access to the game on your device may see its saved entries.

Online photos and third-party services

When online, the game requests artist information and photos from Wikipedia and Wikimedia services. These requests include the requested artist name or image resource, and the services receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser or device request headers. Player names and bidding records are not included in those requests. Requests use HTTPS. These services process information under the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. Following filmography or photo-credit links also connects you to those services.

The companion website is hosted through ChatGPT Sites. The hosting service may process connection information, including IP addresses and technical request logs, to deliver and protect the website, under its applicable privacy policy. Google Play separately processes downloads, purchases if applicable, and platform diagnostics under Google's policies; the game currently has no in-app purchases.

Accounts, permissions, ads and analytics

The game has no account registration or login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated tracking service. It does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or your photo library. Internet access is used for online images and linked information. We do not sell game data or use it for targeted advertising.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are processed to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Messages are handled through the email provider and are retained while needed to resolve your request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address above.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local game data remains until you replace a saved game or clear the application's storage. Starting a new game replaces that industry's saved game but does not clear other industries or cached photo preferences. To remove all local Android game data, use Android Settings → Apps → Cinema Tycoon Bid → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device). For the web version, clear this website's site data in your browser. This removes saved progress and preferences.

Your operating system may back up or restore application data according to your device and backup settings. You control those backups through your device or backup provider. We cannot remotely access or erase your local saves or backup copies.

Security and children

Online content requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Local saves rely on your device and browser protections; avoid entering sensitive information and secure your device. The game does not request children's personal information or provide public messaging or user profiles. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data practices change. Privacy questions and support requests can be sent to cinematycoonbid@gmail.com.
