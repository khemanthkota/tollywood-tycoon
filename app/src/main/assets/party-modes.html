Test without Google Play

  1. In Android Studio, connect your Android phone by USB and press Run to install the debug app.
  2. Connect the host phone and a second phone to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  3. On the host, open Cinema Tycoon Bid multiplayer and tap Host Game.
  4. On the second phone, scan the host's join QR code or open its local join URL in a browser.
  5. Place a test bid and confirm both screens update. Test disconnecting and reconnecting.

Use a real Android phone as the host. The emulator's virtual network may not be reachable from your physical phone. Casting mirrors the host display; it does not create a separate private captain screen.
